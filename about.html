WHO WE ARE

Making Student Accommodation Easier

StayFinder is a student-focused accommodation platform designed to make finding a place to stay easier and more convenient.

We understand that finding suitable accommodation can be challenging, especially for students who are moving closer to their schools or universities.

Our platform brings accommodation information together so students can explore rooms, compare prices and contact accommodation providers.
